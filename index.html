Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Rai Cement.

What is the difference between 32.5R and 42.5N? +
32.5R is ideal for masonry, plastering and general construction. 42.5N is a higher-strength cement meant for structural applications such as columns, beams and slabs.
Do you deliver across Kenya? +
Yes. We deliver nationwide. Free delivery is available on bulk orders of 100 bags and above. Contact us for delivery charges to your location.
Where is your factory / depot located? +
We are located at Athi River, off Mombasa Road, Machakos County. Easy access from Nairobi and the Mombasa highway.
How can I get a quotation? +
Use the Quotation form on this website, or call / WhatsApp us on +254 783 386 550. We respond quickly with pricing and delivery options.
Is Rai Cement KEBS certified? +
Yes. Rai Cement is manufactured by National Cement Co Ltd and meets KEBS standards for quality and performance.
